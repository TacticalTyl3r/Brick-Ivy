Owner-only role permissions

The Discord Owner role always has full access. The Realtor role starts with reservation management only; additional access must be granted here. Permissions from multiple roles combine. How role permissions work

Roles are checked against the live Brick & Ivy Discord server. The Owner role cannot be edited and always has full access. A role that is saved here grants its members Staff Portal access. Multiple role grants combine; removing a role's rule revokes that role's website access unless the user has another eligible role. Managed roles and @everyone cannot be configured.
